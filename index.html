Who We Are

A second-generation firm
built on institutional trust.

Miller³ Consulting was founded on a simple premise: public institutions deserve the same caliber of procurement intelligence that private markets take for granted. For over four decades, we've helped governments, transit authorities, and health systems build systems that are compliant — and competitive.

"We help institutions understand why their systems produce the outcomes they do — and what it takes to change them."

From the first MWBE programs of the 1980s to today's race-neutral analytics, our expertise spans the full arc of public procurement reform — not as legacy, but as living institutional knowledge.
