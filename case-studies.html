Disparity Study & Supplier Diversity Advisory
Serving as a trusted, objective advisor through complex community conversations and board-level scrutiny.

Disparity StudyMWBE ProgramPublic Hearing
Disparity Study (Multiple)
Delivering back-to-back disparity studies that guided MWBE program design for one of the nation's largest school systems.

Miller³ conducted two successive disparity studies for Miami-Dade County Public Schools — one of the largest school systems in the United States. Both studies were presented publicly, including at a major public hearing featuring the Superintendent, and provided the evidentiary foundation for the district's minority business participation programs.

The work demonstrated Miller³'s ability to manage high-profile, publicly scrutinized engagements with the rigor and integrity that large institutional clients require.

Key Outcomes
  • Two legally defensible disparity studies delivered
  • Public hearing presentation to district leadership and community
  • MWBE program design recommendations adopted
